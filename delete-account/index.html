Delete your Happy Notes account

Happy Notes lets signed-in users permanently delete their account and notes from the app. These instructions are available without signing in.

How to request deletion

  1. Open Happy Notes and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm your identity and confirm the permanent deletion.

What deletion removes

The Happy Notes account and all of its notes are deleted permanently and immediately.

What deletion does not remove

Copies already posted to Telegram, Mastodon, Fanfou, or Ember are not removed.
